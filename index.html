Support

Questions, bug reports or feedback — email zintex.ou@gmail.com and a person will reply, usually within two business days. Include your device model, iOS version and, for a bug, what you did just before it happened.

A purchase did not unlock

Sign in with the Apple Account you bought with, then use Restore purchases in the game. Still stuck? Email us the order ID from your Apple receipt.

Cancel a subscription

iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or long-press the app icon → Manage Subscription. Deleting the game does not cancel it. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Lost progress

If you created an account, sign in with the same method (Apple or email) and your career is restored. Otherwise email us when you last played and your device model.

Delete my data

Delete your account in the game — this removes the account and online saves — or email us and we will do it. Deleting the game removes what is stored on your device.
