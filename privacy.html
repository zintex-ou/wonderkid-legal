Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Wonderkid: Rise to Legend (“the game”, “we”) is published by Zintex OÜ (Estonia, zintex.dev). This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and what choices you have. We do not show ads in the game and we do not sell your personal data.

What we use

Account (optional). You can play without an account. If you create one — with Sign in with Apple or an email address — we store your email address (or the private relay address Apple gives us) and a technical account identifier so you can sign in and recover your career on other devices.

Career. Your player, progress, choices, statistics and saves. They are stored on your device and, if you have an account or play online modes with friends, on our servers.

Purchases. The product, the status of your subscription or lifetime purchase, and transaction, renewal, expiry or refund dates — needed to unlock, restore and verify what you bought. We never receive your payment card details.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, we store a push token for your device to send you game reminders and updates about multiplayer careers you take part in. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

Technical data. App version, device model and the diagnostic information strictly needed to run the game and fix errors.

No tracking

The game does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, does not access the advertising identifier and contains no advertising or attribution SDKs.

Service providers

Data is processed only as needed by: Apple (distribution, purchases, Sign in with Apple, notifications); Supabase (accounts, online saves and multiplayer); RevenueCat (verifying and managing purchases — if you have an account, its internal identifier may be linked to your RevenueCat profile so your purchases follow you across devices). Each provider processes data according to its role and its own privacy commitments, and may process it outside the EU/EEA under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Legal bases

Where EU/EEA or UK data-protection law applies: providing the game, saving your career and running your account are necessary to provide the service you asked for; activating, restoring and verifying purchases are necessary to perform the contract and prevent fraud; security, fixing errors and answering support requests rely on our legitimate interest in a reliable service.

Retention

Account and career data are kept while your account is active, and deleted or anonymised within a reasonable time after you delete your account or ask us to, unless the law requires otherwise. Data stored only on your device stays there until you delete the game. Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as long as needed to provide your purchases and meet legal obligations.

Children

The game is not designed to knowingly collect personal data from children below the age of digital consent in their country without the required consent. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your personal data, and object to certain processing. You can delete your account in the game or by emailing us. You can also complain to a supervisory authority — in Estonia, the Andmekaitse Inspektsioon.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes will be announced in the game.
