WONDERKID

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Wonderkid: Rise to Legend (formerly “Projet Footeux”; “the game”, “we”) is published by Zintex OÜ, Ahtri tn 12, Tallinn 15551, Estonia. This policy explains what data the game uses and why. The short version: we store what is needed to keep your game progress and verify your purchases — nothing more, no ads, no tracking.

Game data

To save your career and let you continue it, the game stores your game progress (your player, clubs, matches, choices and in-game items) together with a random identifier for your installation or account. This data is stored with our hosting provider Supabase (Supabase Inc.). If you choose a player or profile name, it is stored with your progress; please do not use your real name or other personal details as a player name.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple through the App Store. To check which purchases are active and to restore them on another device, the game uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat Inc.). RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase receipt, a random identifier generated by the game (not linked to your name or email), basic device and app information (model, OS and app version, language, country/region) and your IP address as part of the network connection. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment card details.

What we do not collect

No advertising identifier — the game does not ask for tracking permission and does not track you across other apps or websites. No contacts, photos, or precise location. We do not show ads, we do not sell or share data, and we do not build a profile of you.

Legal basis and processors

Where EU/EEA or UK data-protection law applies, storing your progress is necessary to provide the game you asked for, and purchase verification is necessary to perform the contract you enter into when you buy. Supabase and RevenueCat act as our processors under their data-processing terms and may process data outside the EU/EEA under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Retention

Game progress is kept while you play and deleted on request, or after 24 months without any activity. Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide and verify your purchases and to meet legal obligations.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country, if higher), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA, UK or another jurisdiction with data-protection law, you have the right to access, correct, export or delete personal data held about you, to object to processing and to complain to a supervisory authority (in Estonia, the Andmekaitse Inspektsioon). Email us and we will help.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes will be announced in the game.
