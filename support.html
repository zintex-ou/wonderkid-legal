Wonderkid: Rise to Legend

Football career game for iPhone

Wonderkid is a football career game: create a young player, make your decisions on and off the pitch, climb through the clubs and try to become a legend. Available for iPhone and iPad.

Frequently asked

I bought something and it did not unlock

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with, then use Restore purchases in the game. If that does not help, email us with the order ID from your Apple receipt.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or long-press the app icon and choose Manage Subscription. Deleting the game does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple.

I lost my progress

If you created an account, sign in with the same method (Apple or email) and your career will be restored. Otherwise email us with roughly when you last played and your device model, and we will try to help.

How do I delete my data?

If you have an account, you can delete it in the game; this removes your account and online saves. You can also email us and we will do it for you. Deleting the game removes what is stored on your device.
